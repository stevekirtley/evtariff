How it works

How the EDF referral works, step by step

This follows EDF's Refer a Friend terms. Nothing to type in: the link itself carries the referral.

01

Open the referral link and enter your postcode. The link looks like edfenergy.com/quote/refer-a-friend/adjective-noun-1234. If it doesn't, it isn't a referral link.

02

Pick a tariff and set up payment. Single fuel or dual fuel both qualify. Not sure which tariff? See which EDF tariff to pick below.

03

EDF switches you from your old supplier. Switches in Great Britain normally complete within a few weeks. You don't need to contact your old supplier.

04

Make your first payment. The reward isn't released until both your first payment and the referrer's have cleared.

05

The credit lands on your account. EDF's terms say usually within 30 days of the later of those two payments. Its FAQ says around four weeks from joining. You both get an email when it's applied.

💡 Top tip: want your credit before your first bill? Pay any amount, even £1, by debit or credit card as soon as your switch completes. You'll get your referral credit the next day.

Got a quote but not ready to switch? EDF emails you the quote. When you come back, start a new quote through the referral link, or the reward won't be matched automatically.

Who qualifies

The catches that stop people getting the credit

Most "my referral never arrived" posts come down to one of these.

EDF customer in the last 12 months

You can't be an EDF customer now, or have been one in the previous 12 months.

Moving into a home already on EDF

The property mustn't currently be supplied by EDF, even if you've never been a customer yourself.

Signing up through a comparison site

The reward is only paid if you sign up directly with EDF through the link, not through Uswitch, MoneySuperMarket and the like.

Stacking with another new-customer offer

Refer a Friend can't be combined with any other new-customer promotion.

Cancelling the switch, or the referrer leaving

If you cancel, or either account is closed before the credit is applied, neither of you gets it.

Referring yourself

You can't refer yourself, including when you move house.

Electricity only, or gas only

Single-fuel switches qualify. One reward is paid per referral either way.

Prepayment meters

Still eligible. Once EDF emails to say the credit is applied, contact them and they'll send it to your meter.

⚠️ Switching by phone? Give the referral link (or the referrer's account email) on your first call. EDF won't add a referral later if it wasn't declared on that call. Forgot the link online? Contact EDF after your first Direct Debit and it can apply it manually.

You don't have an EV

Your choice is between EDF's tracker, its fixed deals and the standard variable tariff (the price cap). EDF's Simply Tracker follows the Ofgem price cap's unit rate and takes 6.85p a day off each fuel's standing charge, about £25 a year per fuel. That's a fixed saving per day, not per kWh, so how much it's worth to you depends on what you're comparing it with.

FAQ

EDF referral questions

Is there an EDF promo code or voucher code?

No. EDF doesn't use promo, voucher or discount codes for new customers. The switching bonus comes from its Refer a Friend scheme: you get a quote through an existing customer's referral link, which looks like edfenergy.com/quote/refer-a-friend/adjective-noun-1234, and the credit is matched to you automatically. Voucher sites that list "EDF codes" are showing referral links or nothing.

What is the EDF referral bonus?

From 16 November 2026 to 5 January 2027, a new customer who joins through a referral link gets £75 of account credit and the person who referred them gets £100. Outside promotions it's normally £50 each. The credit goes on your EDF account and can't be withdrawn as cash.

When does the EDF referral credit arrive?

EDF's terms say rewards are usually paid within 30 days of the later of the two first payments: yours and the referrer's. In practice EDF says it typically takes around four weeks from when you join, and both of you get an email when it's applied. EDF won't answer queries about a missing reward before that window has passed.

Who can use an EDF referral link?

You must not be an EDF customer now or in the last 12 months, the property you're switching must not already be supplied by EDF, and it must be a residential property. You have to sign up directly with EDF through the link, not through a comparison site, and the referral can't be combined with any other new-customer offer.

Does the EDF referral work with Go Electric and other EV tariffs?

Yes. You switch to EDF through the referral link and land on its standard variable tariff first, then move to Go Electric from your online account or the app once EDF can see half-hourly readings from your smart meter. The referral credit is paid on the switch, not on the tariff you end up on.

Does an electricity-only switch qualify for the EDF referral?

Yes. EDF's terms cover signing up for the supply of electricity or gas, so a single-fuel switch qualifies. Only one reward is paid per referral, whether you move one fuel or two.

I forgot to use the referral link. Can I still get the credit?

Possibly. EDF says to contact them and it can apply the credit manually once your first Direct Debit has been taken. If you switch by phone, you must give the referral link or the referrer's email address on that first call; EDF won't add a referral later if it wasn't declared then.

I'm on a prepayment meter. Can I get the EDF referral credit?

Yes. Once you get EDF's email confirming the credit has been applied, contact EDF and it will arrange to send the credit to your meter.
