Your setup

Several of these tariffs have entry requirements. Answer these and the table only shows what you could actually switch to.

Do you have a smart meter sending half-hourly readings? Every tariff here needs one. If you are not sure, your supplier’s app will say.
Is your car or charger on the supplier’s compatible list? Needed to join the smart-dispatch tariffs — Intelligent Octopus Go and E.ON Next Drive Smart. Tesla, VW Group, BMW/Mini, Ford, Polestar and chargers like Ohme, Hypervolt, Zappi and Indra are usually covered.
Do you have a Pod Point charger, or would you buy one? EDF’s Pod Power tariffs need Pod Point hardware. Plug & Power bundles the charger into the standing charge.
Did you get your EV through Octopus Electric Vehicles? Their lease and salary-sacrifice scheme. It unlocks the EV Saver version of Intelligent Octopus Go, which is cheaper overnight than the standard one.
Do you work for EDF? EDF’s Engage EV tariffs are for their own employees.
