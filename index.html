About this site

Who wrote this?

I'm Steve Kirtley, a developer and EV driver based in Wales. I switched from Octopus Intelligent Go to EDF Go Electric in May 2026 after Octopus raised prices and began enforcing the 6-hour dispatch cap.

The piece that was missing was a HomeAssistant integration for EDF — so I built one. HomeAssistant-EDFEnergy is a fork of BottleCapDave's excellent Octopus integration, adapted for EDF's API. It's MIT Licensed and open to contributions.

All rates on this site are taken directly from supplier APIs and baked in at time of writing. Always verify current rates before switching. EDF referral links earn me £100 and currently give you a £75 switching bonus (promotional rate until 13 July 2026).
