The reward

£100 each: what the Fuse referral pays now

When you join Fuse with a customer's referral code during the current campaign, you get £100 of account credit and so does the person who referred you. The campaign on my code runs until 14 December 2026. Fuse support confirmed this to me on 21 November 2026.

You'll see other figures around, including on Fuse's own referral programme page, which mentions £50. That's because Fuse runs referrals as separate campaigns. A reward keeps the rules that were in force when the code was entered, so older referrals can show a different amount. Who qualifies

The catches that stop people getting the credit

A previous Fuse contract

You must never have been a Fuse customer before.

Switching through a comparison site

Uswitch, GoCompare and similar sign-ups are excluded. Go to Fuse directly.

Paying the first bill late

The first full month must be paid in full within 14 days of the bill, or the reward fails.

A referrer in debt

If the person whose code you use has a negative Fuse balance, neither of you gets the credit.

Export-only sign-ups

Selling solar electricity to Fuse on its own doesn't count. You need an import electricity or gas supply.

Adding the code later

Missed it at sign-up? Add it in the app under Referrals, while the campaign is running.

Gas only, or electricity only

A single-fuel switch qualifies for the same reward as dual fuel.

Keeping your other fuel elsewhere

You can move just your gas to Fuse and keep electricity with another supplier.

What Fuse offers for gas

Fuse's gas prices change almost daily and it has no public price feed, so rather than a table that's out of date by tomorrow, here's the shape of what it sells. My May tariff is no longer available to new customers.

  • Several fixes at once: when I checked in November 2026 there were 12, 16, 18 and 24-month fixes, plus a 15-month tracker and a variable tariff.
  • Exit fees are modest: £50 on most fixes, £100 on the 15-month tariffs, nothing on the variable.
  • Longer isn't always dearer: the 18-month fix came out cheaper than the 12-month at my usage, with the same £50 exit fee.
  • Standing charges vary a lot between tariffs: from about 13p to 30p a day. If you use little gas, compare standing charges first; if you use a lot, the unit rate matters more.
  • Prepayment is available too, by contacting Fuse support.

Fuse's quote shows an estimated annual cost for every tariff side by side, at your usage, which is the fairest way to compare them. Do that, then check the exit fee on the one you pick.

Splitting fuels means two referral credits. Each supplier accepts a single-fuel switch, so you can join EDF for electricity with an EDF referral link and Fuse for gas with a Fuse code, and get the credit from both.

The catch with splitting is two accounts, two apps and two Direct Debits. Some suppliers also price dual fuel lower than two single fuels, so compare the total, not just one fuel.

Before you switch to Fuse, compare

  • Your estimated annual cost at your own usage, not just the credit
  • Unit rates and daily standing charges for each fuel
  • Fixed or variable, how long the fix runs, and the exit fees
  • Whether you're happy managing your account mainly through an app
FAQ

Fuse referral questions

What is the Fuse Energy referral reward?

£100 of account credit for you and £100 for the person who referred you, when the code is entered during the current campaign, which runs until 14 December 2026. It's a flat amount per customer, whether you switch electricity, gas or both. The reward follows the rules in force when you entered the code, so older referrals can show a different amount.

Is there a Fuse Energy discount code?

No, not in the usual sense. Fuse's switching credit comes from customer referral codes like mine, STEVE25217, which you enter during sign-up or add afterwards in the app. Only one code can go on an account, so make sure it's the one you want.

Where do I enter a Fuse referral code?

Towards the end of Fuse's sign-up, after you've added your property. If you missed it, you can add it afterwards: in the Fuse app, go to Referrals and tap "Tap to add a code" next to "Were you invited to Fuse?". Do it while the referral campaign is still running.

When does the Fuse referral credit arrive?

After you pay your first Fuse bill in full, within 14 days of it being issued. Until then the reward shows as Pending in the app. If the first bill isn't paid in time it shows as Failed. The credit is offset against later bills and can't be taken as cash.

Does a gas-only switch to Fuse qualify for the referral?

Yes. Fuse's terms say the reward is the same whether you switch import electricity only, gas only, or both. Export-only sign-ups (selling solar electricity to Fuse) don't qualify.

Can I have gas with Fuse and electricity with another supplier?

Yes. Gas and electricity are separate supplies and can be with different suppliers. That's what I do: gas with Fuse, electricity with EDF on its Go Electric EV tariff. Each supplier's referral scheme accepts a single-fuel switch.

Can I use a Fuse referral code through a comparison site?

No. Fuse excludes customers who switch through price comparison websites such as Uswitch or GoCompare. Get your quote and sign up directly on the Fuse website or app.
